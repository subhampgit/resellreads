BUILT BY READERS.

Student-run. Reader-driven.

Resell Reads is a student-run bookstore and reading community created to make reading more accessible and affordable.

We sell both brand-new and pre-loved books, offer affordable softcopies, and are building a community library where books can move from one reader to another.

We started small, but our goal is simple: help more people discover great books without having to spend a fortune.

For students. By students.
